Where People Wait and Buy Snacks

Bus shelters are little beacons of everyday life—queues, quick bites, coffee, umbrellas on rainy days. They hint at steady foot traffic, the kind that keeps small vendors afloat.

Takeaway: Compare these dots with the “no-go” layers. Are we pushing vendors away from where riders actually are?

Parks as Neighborhood Living Rooms

Parks and recreation properties cover a wide range of community spaces. These sites host weekend tournaments, summer camps, or casual family outings, and serve as everyday “third places.”

Takeaway: With vending rules overlaid on these park units, the real question is whether small, low-cost services can be part of the park experience—or are pushed away from the very spaces where people gather.
