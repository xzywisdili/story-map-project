About This Story Map

Street vending rules & everyday spaces in Philadelphia

Why This Map?

Philadelphia’s vendors navigate a patchwork of prohibited zones, special districts, and small exceptions. This map visualizes those rules alongside everyday demand signals like bus stops and parks.

Who It’s For

Jasmine, a new food cart operator—she needs to quickly see where vending is legal and where customers actually gather.
